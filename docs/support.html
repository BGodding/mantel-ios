Help with Mantel

Mantel sends photos and videos to a private Nextcloud frame folder set up by your family administrator. Most questions come down to one of these.

Common questions

"Invalid credentials" when I sign in

Use an app password, not your regular Nextcloud login password. Ask whoever administers your Nextcloud server to generate one for you under Settings → Security → App passwords.

I don't see any frames to send photos to

Frames are folders your administrator shares with your account, with permission to add files. Ask them to check the share, then pull to refresh the frame list in Mantel.

A file with this name already exists

Mantel never overwrites a file with the same name — this is Nextcloud protecting existing files. Rename the item on your phone, or ask your administrator about the existing copy.

An upload says "will retry" and doesn't finish

Large videos upload in the background and resume automatically once you have a connection — you can close the app and it keeps going. If it's been stuck a long time, check that your phone can reach your Nextcloud server at all (try opening it in Safari).

The photo gallery or delete option isn't showing up

Both are optional features your administrator turns on server-side. If you expect to have them, check with your administrator.

Still stuck?

Write to us and mention your Nextcloud username (never your password) and what you were trying to do.
