Mantel
Privacy Policy

Your photos go to your server. That's the whole policy.

Effective September 15, 2026  ·  Applies to the Mantel iOS app, version 1.0

At a glance

✓

Your photos and videos go only to the Nextcloud server your family administrator configured — never to Mantel's developer.

✓

Your username and app password live only in your iPhone's Keychain, encrypted by iOS. We never see them.

✕

No advertising, no analytics SDK, no selling of data. Mantel isn't free-with-ads software — it's a private family tool.

✕

No account with us. You sign in to your own Nextcloud server; Mantel's developer has no account system at all.

01

What Mantel is

Mantel is a private uploader for digital photo frames. A family administrator runs a Nextcloud server and shares a folder with you — one folder per frame, like "Grandma's Kitchen" or "The Lake House." Mantel lets you pick photos and videos from your phone and send them to that folder. It is not a public app: you need a Nextcloud account and an invitation from an administrator to use it at all.

02

What we collect, and where it actually goes

Your Nextcloud credentials. You enter a username and an app-specific password (never your main Nextcloud login password). This is stored only in your device's Keychain, protected by iOS, and is sent only as an authentication header to the Nextcloud server address your administrator configured — over an encrypted connection. It is never transmitted to Mantel's developer, Apple, or anyone else.

The photos and videos you choose to send. When you pick items from the share sheet or the in-app picker, Mantel uploads them directly to your configured Nextcloud server over an encrypted connection. Mantel's developer never receives, stores, views, or has any access to your photos or videos — they go from your device to your server and nowhere else.

A random install identifier, for turning features on and off. Once per launch, Mantel checks Firebase Remote Config (a Google service) to see which optional features — like the photo gallery view — are currently enabled. This sends a random identifier tied to this one app install (not your name, email, or Nextcloud account) plus basic app and iOS version numbers. No photos, credentials, or personal content are included in this check.

On-device diagnostics. Mantel uses Apple's built-in MetricKit to watch for crashes and slow performance. These reports stay in your device's own diagnostic logs — Mantel does not transmit them to its developer or to any server.

03

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad-tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service beyond Apple's own on-device MetricKit (§02).
  • We never sell, rent, or share your data — we don't have your data to begin with.
  • No account, profile, or mailing list with Mantel's developer. Your only account is the Nextcloud account your administrator created for you.

04

Camera roll and photo access

Mantel uses Apple's PHPickerViewController and the system share sheet to let you choose photos. Both are privacy-preserving by design: Mantel is only ever given the specific items you select — it is never granted, and never requests, access to browse your full photo library.

05

Deleting your data

Deleting the Mantel app removes everything it stored on your device, including your saved credentials. Photos already uploaded live on your Nextcloud server under your administrator's control — removing them from a frame's folder, or asking your administrator to revoke your share, removes your access and (where the delete feature is enabled) removes the file itself.

06

Children's privacy

Mantel is not directed at children and has no open public sign-up — every user is individually invited by a family administrator who already controls the underlying server and its data.

07

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Meaningful changes to what we collect will be reflected here before they ship in an app update.
